# ShrinkDrop privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

ShrinkDrop converts and shrinks photos and videos **entirely on your PC**.

- **No data leaves your computer.** ShrinkDrop does not upload, send or share your files or any information about them.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.**
- **What is stored:** your ShrinkDrop settings (size, quality, output folder) in `%APPDATA%\ShrinkDrop` on your PC. Your original files are only read; the new files are saved where you choose.
- **Microsoft Store version:** to check your trial or purchase, Windows contacts the Microsoft Store. That is handled by Windows under [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); ShrinkDrop receives only whether the app is licensed.
