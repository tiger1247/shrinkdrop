## Description

ShrinkDrop turns iPhone photos into JPGs that open everywhere, and shrinks big videos so you can email or share them. Everything happens on your PC, so nothing is uploaded.

**Convert HEIC to JPG**
iPhone photos are saved as HEIC, which many apps and websites can't open. Drop them on ShrinkDrop, or a whole folder, and get JPGs in seconds. Camera details, colour and dates are kept.

**Shrink videos to fit**
Pick a size, like 25 MB for email or 10 MB for Discord, and ShrinkDrop makes the video fit. Phone videos usually shrink by 80–95%, and portrait videos stay the right way up.

**Shrink photos**
Resize photos to 4K, 2048 or 1600 pixels and choose the quality, so they're quick to upload and share.

- Drag and drop files or folders, or right-click → Share → ShrinkDrop
- Your originals are never changed
- No account, no watermark, no ads
- Works offline on Windows 10 and 11
